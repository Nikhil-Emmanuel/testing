I have started this a hundred times.

Every version began by defending myself.

So I deleted every one of them.

This one doesn’t.

01 · Then

Before I made it hard

There was a version of us where you laughed easily and told me things the moment you thought them. You weren’t careful around me yet.

I want you to know I remember exactly what that felt like — because I’m the reason it stopped.

02 · Where it turned

I stopped listening

Somewhere along the way I started hearing your words and missing everything underneath them. You would tell me you were hurting and I’d hear it but didnt do anything about it.

You weren’t asking me to be right. You were asking me to understand, and be emotionally pro-active and intelligent, and I gave you neither.

03 · The hard part

What it cost you

You made yourself isolated so that you dont get hurt by expectations. You started feeling peaceful and safe when you stopped sharing your feelings with me. I was the reason.

You carried us and our relationship alone with determination and called it love. That is what I did to you, and there is no gentle way for me to say it.

04 · No excuses

The reasons aren’t yours to carry

I could hand you explanations. Pressure, timing, the way I was taught to handle things I couldn’t control.

All of them explain me. None of them excuse what I did to you. You were never the problem I made you feel like.

05 · Now

What’s actually different

Not promises — I’ve spent enough of your patience on those. Things I have already started doing, that you can check.

They’re further down this page. Read them with an open mind.

06 · Terminus

Mysore Road

I can’t open it early and neither can you. That’s the point — I don’t want to talk you into anything today.

Not Promises · Commitments

Things you can hold me to

Words got us here. So these are specific enough that you’ll know immediately if I break them.

  1. I will remember the details to the best of my knowledge. I wont give any excuses that i forgot, because i want to remember the beautiful moments with you and be your comfort for the sad ones.

  2. When you tell me I hurt you, my first sentence isn’t a defence. No “but”, no context, no explaining what I meant. I listen all the way to the end first.

  3. I’m working on my emotions so that i understand better. It stopped being fair to make you the person who teaches me the bare minimums.

  4. Your no is a full answer. I don’t negotiate it, wear it down, or come back at it from another angle.

  5. You never have to shrink or compromise to keep the peace with me again. If you feel yourself doing it, say the word and I stop whatever I’m doing.

I’m not asking you to forgive me today.

I’m asking you to believe that I finally see it — clearly, without needing you to explain it to me one more time. Whatever you decide after that is yours, and I’ll respect it completely.

I’ll be on that platform at 12:15, with the letter, whether or not you come. There’s no version of this where you owe me your presence.

Nikhil
